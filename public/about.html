How it works

  1. Describe it. Open Tools and write a few sentences: "A podcast and short videos lead to a free guide. A welcome sequence invites people to a live workshop, then to a membership."
  2. Read the map. Each piece becomes a card with a category, a one-line description, an owner and a status. Arrows show where people go next.
  3. Change it in words. Choose Update this map and write what should change: "Add a referral loop after the workshop." Cards you edited by hand keep your edits.
  4. Change it by hand. Click a card to rename it or set its owner. Drag from one card to another to connect them. Undo steps back through every change, including the ones the AI made.
  5. Show it. Present hides the editing panels. Export JSON saves the map as a file you can keep or send.

What the cards mean

AudienceWho you serve.
ContentWhat people see first: a podcast, videos, posts.
CaptureWhere a stranger becomes a contact: a guide, a quiz, a signup.
NurtureWhat keeps the conversation going: emails, a community.
OfferWhat people can buy.
DeliveryHow a buyer gets what they paid for.
TeamWho does the work.
SystemThe tools underneath.

Every new card starts with the status "idea." You set it to "building" or "live" when that is true.

Where your map lives

The map and its saved copies stay in this browser. Nothing is stored on a server. When you ask the AI for a map, your description and the current map are sent to the AI model for that one request. Export JSON to keep a copy or to open the map on another device.
